The Hidden Friction

Freedom shouldn’t come with a safety tradeoff.

For many women, traveling alone is one of the most powerful ways to experience independence, self-discovery, and the world on their own terms.

Yet solo travel also comes with a constant layer of invisible decision-making:

  • 01 Should I walk back alone?
  • 02 Should I share my location?
  • 03 Does someone know when I should arrive?
  • 04 Is this route safe?
  • 05 Am I overreacting, or should I ask for help?
  • 06 What happens when I feel uncomfortable—but it is not yet an emergency?

Most safety tools are designed for the final moment: an emergency. Gate 27 is interested in everything that happens before that moment.

Technology & Hardware Layer

Meet Tulihood

Safety, beauty, and technology woven into the journey.

Tulihood Smart Travel Systems is the product and technology layer powering Gate 27. Instead of asking women to carry another obvious safety device, Tulihood explores how safety can be embedded into something they already choose to carry.

Physical Hardware Concept The Hardware

The Companion Bag

The first concept is a connected travel companion combining a fashion-forward crossbody/travel bag with lightweight tactile safety interactions.

  • ✦ Discreet Tactile Safety Trigger: Quick, concealed tactile activation without looking at your screen.
  • ✦ Secure / Anti-Theft Storage: Concealed locking compartments protecting cards and passports.
  • ✦ Travel-Friendly Organization: Purpose-built compartments for currencies, passports, and tech essentials.
  • ✦ Phone-Connected Interaction: Low-power pairing with gentle haptic reassurance feedback.
  • ✦ Lightweight Everyday Design: Premium aesthetics suitable for museums, night transit, and dining.
  • ✦ Optional Future Tulipy Charm: Tactile charm interaction point for emotional identity.
* Early-stage concept in prototype development. Not currently manufactured or commercially sold.
Digital Software Concept The App Layer

The Gate 27 App

A minimalist, highly focused mobile companion experience designed specifically for solo travelers' real-world safety flow.

  • ✦ Trusted Circle: Select and curate the specific people you trust before heading out.
  • ✦ Smart Check-In: Set an expected arrival window when embarking on new or late routes.
  • ✦ Location Sharing: Share your live location privately when needed with a single discreet action.
  • ✦ Safety Escalation: Quietly alert a trusted contact if your check-in window passes.
  • ✦ Travel Status: Let your circle know you arrived safely with one tap closing the loop.
  • ✦ Emergency Mode: Seamlessly escalate support when a situation becomes serious.
* Focused MVP architecture: cleanly omitting clutter like social feeds, AI trip planning, or crypto.

Before It Becomes an Emergency

Why Gate 27 is built around nuanced support rather than just a basic panic button.

  • Set an arrival check-in.
  • Your Trusted Circle knows your expected arrival.
  • When you arrive, one tap closes the loop.
SCENARIO 02

Something feels off

“You are walking back at night and feel that someone may be following you, but you are not sure.”

  • Use a discreet interaction on the bag.
  • Begin location sharing without visibly taking out your phone.
  • Connect to someone you trust.

A situation escalates

“You determine that you need immediate help.”

  • Mobilize your Trusted Circle immediately.

The Founder Story

It started at a gate.

Gate 27 grew from my own experience traveling alone.

During a night tour in Doha, I met another young woman who was also traveling solo. We came from different places and had never met before, yet during those few hours we became each other’s familiarity, confidence, and sense of connection in an unfamiliar city.

When we left Doha through different airport gates, I kept thinking about the contradiction at the center of solo travel: Traveling alone can make you feel incredibly free—and incredibly aware that you are alone.

The first version of the idea was a soft travel companion, Tulipy, designed around emotional reassurance. As the concept evolved, I began asking a deeper question: Could emotional reassurance and functional safety exist in the same everyday object?

That question became Gate 27 and Tulihood Smart Travel Systems. What began as a companion is becoming a system for confidence, connection, and freedom of movement.

Our Philosophy

Breaking invisible boundaries.

For women, freedom of movement is not always experienced equally. The boundaries are rarely physical.

Sometimes they are: the street you avoid, the train you do not take, the hour you decide is too late, the destination you reconsider, or the message you send to someone just to say, “I made it back.”

Gate 27 is not about telling women where they should or should not go. It is about designing better support for the women who choose to go anyway.

“Safety should expand freedom. Not define its limits.”
